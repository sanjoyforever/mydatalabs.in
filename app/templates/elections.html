Seats — change in projected NDA seats, out of 543. Sentiment — change in the composite sentiment index, a −100 to +100 scale built from the weighted survey answers. It is what drives the seat number, so the two always move together. z — the move divided by how much this series normally drifts over any 30 days. Below ±1 is ordinary wandering.
📌 Read this as association, not causation. The model has no event variable — it only sees survey answers, so an impact here means the electorate's answers moved across that window, indicating potentially the event may have caused the move.

          2019 & 2024 General Election Backtest

          The model is run on the mean survey reading over the 60 days before each result date, then scored against the actual outcome. Numbers below are computed live, not stored.

          📌 How to read this: The majority mark is 272 of 543. The vote-share fit is anchored on both elections, so neither year is a true out-of-sample test of that step. The votes-to-seats exponent is fitted jointly across both, which is why 2024 is no longer reproduced exactly: a single exponent has to serve two very different alliance structures.
          Data source

          Every projection on this page is derived from the daily public opinion trackers published by CVoter India. CVoter runs the fieldwork, the sampling and the daily surveys; all 41 labelled answer series used here come from their published trackers. The modelling, weighting and seat projection are MyDataLabs' own and are not endorsed by, affiliated with, or produced in collaboration with CVoter.

          Source data: cvoterindia.com

          About this projection

          543-seat Lok Sabha projection from a weighted composite of CVoter's option-level survey responses, calibrated against the 2019 and 2024 general election results. Backtest error against those two elections averages 5.3 seats.

          This is a model, not a forecast of any announced election. It carries no seat-level or state-level contest data and cannot see alliance arithmetic or candidate effects.
