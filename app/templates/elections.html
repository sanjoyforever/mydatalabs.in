          Data source

          Every projection on this page is derived from the daily public opinion trackers published by CVoter India. CVoter runs the fieldwork, the sampling and the daily surveys; all 41 labelled answer series used here come from their published trackers. The modelling, weighting and seat projection are MyDataLabs' own and are not endorsed by, affiliated with, or produced in collaboration with CVoter.

          Source data: cvoterindia.com

          About this projection

          543-seat Lok Sabha projection from a weighted composite of CVoter's option-level survey responses, calibrated against the 2019 and 2024 general election results. Backtest error against those two elections averages 5.3 seats.

          This is a model, not a forecast of any announced election. It carries no seat-level or state-level contest data and cannot see alliance arithmetic or candidate effects.

          {% include "_elections_methodology.html" %} {# The backtest matrix sits with the methodology, not the dashboard: it is a statement about how the model was fitted and how far it missed, which is what the rest of this tab documents. On the dashboard it read as one more current-conditions panel, which it is not — nothing in it changes when today's survey moves. #}

          2019 & 2024 general election backtest

          The model is run on the mean survey reading over the 60 days before each result date, then scored against the actual outcome. Computed on each update, not stored by hand.

          How to read this: The majority mark is 272 of 543. Both the vote-share fit and the votes-to-seats exponent are anchored on these two elections, so neither year is an out-of-sample test. A single exponent has to serve two very different alliance structures, which is why 2024 is not reproduced exactly.
