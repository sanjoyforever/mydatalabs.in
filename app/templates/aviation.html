Mathematical Architecture & Methodology

The Airline Pressure Index (API-INDEX) measures global commercial aviation stress across operational, energy, supply-chain, and currency dimensions.

Composite Scoring Formula

API-INDEX = 100.0 + (∑ [ Weight_i × Stress_i ] − 38.0) × 1.35

Each component stress score is normalized between 0.0 (calm equilibrium) and 100.0 (crisis cap). 100.0 represents the non-crisis operating baseline.
