4. Independent Projection Engine Disclaimer

The Lok Sabha Projection Engine (LS-PROJ) is an independent statistical model utilizing Monte Carlo simulations, historical electoral baselines, and public opinion tracking data.

Seat projections represent probabilistic statistical estimates, not official election results or official forecasts issued by election commissions or government bodies.

MyDataLabs conducts no fieldwork, interviews no respondents and does not carry out any exit poll. LS-PROJ is a re-analysis of survey results already published by third parties. Where Section 126A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, or any direction of the Election Commission of India restricts the conduct, publication or dissemination of exit polls or opinion polls for a defined period, publication of the affected projections is suspended for that period.

The projection is a statistical estimate of present public opinion projected onto a hypothetical immediate poll. It is not a prediction of any announced or unannounced election, is not affiliated with or endorsed by any political party, candidate, or the Election Commission of India, and must not be represented as an official or semi-official forecast.
