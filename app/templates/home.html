Methodological principles

How MyDataLabs indices are designed, calculated, and maintained.

01

Open Methodology

Every index publishes its exact mathematical formula, component weights, normalization methods, and source attributions. No black boxes.

02

Fixed Historical Baselines

Scores are anchored to empirical non-crisis baseline periods, not arbitrary rolling windows, so a 140 today means the same as a 140 next year.

03

Weekly Cadence

Indices update on a weekly cadence, smoothing daily market noise while capturing structural dislocations as they develop.

04

Automated & Auditable

Data pipelines fetch, validate, and compute scores automatically with full historical audit trails and provenance records.
