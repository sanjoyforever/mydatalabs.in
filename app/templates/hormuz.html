Quantitative Index Methodology

The Hormuz Crisis Index (HMX-INDEX) measures composite geopolitical, maritime and economic stress relative to a calm baseline period ({{ baseline_window|default('January 2026') }}).

Index Score = 100.0 + ∑ [ Weighti × StressScore( Currenti, Baselinei, Capi ) ]
Normalization & capping: each indicator's percentage change from baseline is normalised to a 0–100 stress scale. Component-specific caps stop a single volatile market from dominating the composite.
Directional inversion: for ship transits a decline below baseline is the crisis signal, so its change is inverted before scoring.
One-sided by design: stress scores floor at zero, so the composite cannot fall below 100.0. The index measures stress against calm, not conditions calmer than calm.
