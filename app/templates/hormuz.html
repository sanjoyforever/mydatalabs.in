Quantitative Index Methodology

The Hormuz Crisis Index (HMX-INDEX) measures composite geopolitical, maritime, and economic stress relative to a calm baseline period (February 1–5, 2026).

Index Score = 100.0 + ∑ [ Weight_i × StressScore( Current_i, Baseline_i, Cap_i ) ]
Normalization & Capping: Each indicator's percentage change from baseline is normalized to a 0–100 stress scale. Capping thresholds prevent single volatile market spikes from blowing out the index.
Directional Inversion: For ship transits, a decline below baseline represents crisis stress and is inverted automatically in the scoring model.
